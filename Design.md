## Loops
We need to make a fun gameplay loop that makes it fun to play the game
### Core gameplay loop
1. Fight boss that is overpowered, and get cooked by it
2. Farm an easier boss or die 100 times to this boss
3. Get its magic with a percentage chance
4. Beat harder bosses

## Key decisions
- There are no levels and no vertical progression
