The core loop is pretty simple. You get cooked by a boss, you keep respawning until it dies, you get its magic or weapon, and then you go cook harder bosses with it.

## Rules
- There are no account levels. The only progress is your magics, your weapons, and your mastery.
- Anyone can fight any boss. When you die you respawn in the arena with 2 seconds of invulnerability, the boss never heals, and it resets after the arena has been empty for 60 seconds.
- Bosses are allowed to be unfair, but never in a way that causes death loops. That means spawn invulnerability, stun immunity, and no spawn camping.
- If you did any damage at all, you're eligible for loot. Drops are personal.
- Endgame damage should end up around 5 to 6 times beginner damage.
- Blessings are either a big damage boost or a support that defines your playstyle, but they should never be better than your magic.
- Anime assets (One Piece etc.) are fine for now. If they get moderated, I'll replace them.
- Every animation, model, sound, and icon is referenced by ID in the config, so I can swap in my own later.

## Tiers
Thunder → Dragon → Titan → God (Gorran) → Celestial → Hypergod → Universal

For now the placeholders are plain R6 rigs, colored by tier, all with the same moveset and more HP each tier.

## Controls
- 1 to 3 are magic moves, 4 is weapon, 5 is blessing, 6 is ultimate
- For weapons and blessings, pressing the key equips it, M1 does the attack string, and V does the special of whatever's equipped
- F blocks, Q dashes, Ctrl runs, M opens the menu

## Loot (every kill)
- Mastery XP, always
- A chance at each magic (Heaven Splitter is 5% from Gorran)
- A chance at the boss's own weapon
- Maybe a blessing roll
- A bonus roll if you killed it without dying (only on easier bosses)

## Mastery
- Caps at 100. It only unlocks moves (at levels 1, 20, 45, and so on).
- XP comes from damage dealt, last hits (which also share XP to the other magics you used), and spare copies.
- It should be really fast. The final move should take about 10 minutes of grinding.

## Misc
- Everyone starts with 5 rolls. Spare copies turn into XP or rolls. No Forget.
- Arcane is removed. Combat will get replaced by a new starter magic later.
- Saves get wiped at v2.0. The game is public.
